Frequently asked questions

Where is the workshop held?

Strange Training, Šámalova 62a, 615 00 Brno-Židenice.

Can I attend just one day of the workshop?

Yes, you can attend one day only. The price for one day is CZK 7,000. If you are interested, contact us at info@radekrosastrong.cz.

How long does the workshop last?

Approximately 7–8 hours each day, depending on the programme and practical sessions.

Can I attend if I am not a coach?

Yes. Previous workshops have also been attended by people who do not coach others professionally. They joined primarily for their own education and a deeper understanding of principles they could use in their own training.

Can I split the payment?

Payment in instalments is not a standard option, but we understand that everyone's circumstances differ. If you are a student, for example, or would find it easier to pay in several instalments, email info@radekrosastrong.cz and we will try to find an individual arrangement.
