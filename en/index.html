Radek Rosa

Strength & Conditioning Coach | Educator

I provide strength and conditioning for professional athletes and the general population. My work brings together scientific knowledge and practical experience from sport. Alongside individual training, I deliver specialist workshops for coaches and physiotherapists.

More about meLess about me

I first discovered strength training while preparing with the Slavia Praha rugby club in 2005. I later competed in several bouts under MMA and Olympic boxing rules. Today, I focus on coaching clients, writing educational publications and organising courses for coaches. More than 700 participants from Czechia and Slovakia have attended my courses. I also occasionally lecture at the Faculty of Physical Education and Sport at Charles University.

I am part of the TopAtlet project and work as a strength and conditioning coach at Reinders MMA. In my ongoing pursuit of knowledge, I have had the opportunity to learn in person from leading specialists around the world, including Cal Dietz, Alex Natera, Pat Davidson, Tim Gabbett and others.
