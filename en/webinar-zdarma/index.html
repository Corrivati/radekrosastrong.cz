Free Webinar: Emotional Intelligence

Emotional intelligence influences whether people trust you, listen to you in challenging situations and want to work with you over the long term. In coaching and working with people generally, it is not just what you say that matters, but how you say it and in what context. This webinar offers a practical perspective on emotional intelligence and how to apply it in everyday work.

Date and location:
Online - 25 February 2026 - 19:00 (Wednesday)

Duration: Approx. 30 minutes
Capacity: Limited

Who it is for:
- Strength and conditioning coaches
- Fitness coaches
- Physiotherapists
- Anyone looking to improve their communication skills

Topics:
- Why EI matters in coaching and beyond
- The importance of EI in the age of artificial intelligence
- How to assess your level of EI (a tool)
- Benefits and drawbacks
- Genetics versus intelligence
- How to develop EI
- Practical tips for working with clients
- How to deepen your relationship with the people you coach
and more...
