An individual approach

Who is personal training for?

Everyone has different goals, experience and movement capabilities. I always tailor training to the individual.

01

Professional and competitive athletes

Strength and conditioning focused on improving athletic performance and meeting the demands of your sport.

02

Recreational trainees

Individual training focused on strength, fitness, body composition and long-term development.

03

Beginners

Learning sound technique and fundamental movement patterns, while gradually developing strength.

04

Experienced trainees

More advanced planning and training optimisation to achieve specific performance or physique goals.

Strength · Conditioning · Movement

How can I help?

  • Developing maximal strength and explosive power
  • Improving physical fitness
  • Enhancing athletic performance
  • Improving exercise technique
  • Building muscle mass
  • Reducing body fat
  • Developing mobility and flexibility
  • A gradual return to training load after injury

Strength and conditioning, movement coaching and a return to training load within the scope of a coach's practice. This is not a medical rehabilitation service.

Step by step

How does the coaching process work?

A clear direction from our first conversation through ongoing evaluation.

I am interested in working together
  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    We will discuss your goals, experience, availability and expectations of training.

  2. 02

    Setting goals

    We will define realistic short- and long-term goals to guide our work together.

  3. 03

    Movement assessment

    We will assess fundamental movement patterns, mobility, stability and any movement limitations within the scope of a coach-led movement screening.

  4. 04

    Personal Training

    We will adapt training to your current performance, capabilities and agreed goals.

  5. 05

    Ongoing evaluation

    We will monitor your development, adjust training load and work progressively towards your goals.
