1. Introductory provisions

Protecting the personal data and privacy of website visitors, clients and participants in educational events is important to me.

This policy explains which personal data I process, the purposes for which I use it, who it may be disclosed to, how long I retain it and the rights of the people whose data is processed.

Personal data is processed in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR) and the related legislation of the Czech Republic.

This policy applies in particular to:

  • website visitors,
  • people enquiring about personal training and online coaching,
  • participants in specialist courses and workshops,
  • people communicating with me by email or through other contact channels,
  • people appearing in photographs and video recordings from educational events I organise,
  • recipients of marketing communications.

2. Personal data I process

The scope of the data depends on how you use my services.

2.1 Course and workshop registration

When you place an order or register, I may process:

  • your first name and surname,
  • your email address,
  • your telephone number, if provided,
  • billing details,
  • the name and date of the course ordered,
  • order and payment status information,
  • information needed to organise your attendance,
  • any details needed to issue confirmation of attendance or a certificate of completion.

2.2 Payments and invoicing

When you pay through the Stripe payment gateway, information needed to complete and verify the transaction may be processed.

As the organiser, I may receive information about the payment, including its amount, status, transaction identifier and relevant billing details.

Full payment card details are not normally disclosed to me as the organiser; they are processed by the payment service provider within the scope of the service used.

2.3 Enquiries and communication

If you contact me about training, online coaching, a workshop or another form of collaboration, I may process:

  • your name and contact details,
  • the content of your message,
  • information about the collaboration you are seeking,
  • related communication needed to deal with your enquiry.

If subsequent coaching involves additional personal data, such as detailed training records or health information, the processing must be assessed against the actual scope of the collaboration. Health data requires compliance with the special conditions under Article 9 GDPR.

2.4 Photographs and video recordings

During educational events, the following may be created:

  • photographs of participants and the instructor,
  • short video recordings,
  • recordings of practical demonstrations,
  • group photographs,
  • materials documenting the educational event.

Where an individual can be identified in a recording and their personal data is processed, the relevant data protection rules apply.

2.5 Data collected when using the website

When you visit the website, technical information may also be processed, such as your IP address, browser and device information, technical server logs and information relating to cookies or similar technologies.

The specific scope depends on the services actually used and the website settings.

4. Photography and filming at educational events

4.1 Purpose of recordings

Photographs and video recordings may be taken at specialist courses and workshops to document the educational event, present the organiser's work and promote further educational events.

Selected materials may be published on www.radekrosastrong.cz and on social media, particularly Instagram and Facebook.

Photographs and videos may also be used to promote future educational events through paid online advertising.

4.2 Protecting participants' privacy

Participants are informed about planned photography and filming in advance or on arrival at the event.

If a participant does not wish to be photographed, filmed or shown in publicly published materials, they may inform me in advance by email at info@radekrosastrong.cz or in person at the venue.

Giving notice in good time helps ensure the participant is not included in planned shots. However, they may also express their wishes later.

Reasonable organisational or technical measures will be taken, such as changing the composition of a photograph, selecting a different shot or anonymising the person's appearance.

4.3 Marketing use of an identifiable likeness

For targeted promotional use of identifiable people, particularly close-up photographs, interviews, testimonial videos or footage used in paid advertising, I will obtain the necessary permission from the people concerned and, where applicable, separate consent under the GDPR in advance.

Such permission or consent is not a condition of attending a workshop.

Registration, workshop attendance or failure to object does not automatically grant permission for unrestricted marketing use of a participant's likeness.

4.4 Withdrawing consent and requesting removal

Where publication is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time by sending a request to info@radekrosastrong.cz.

I will assess and handle requests to remove or amend published materials in accordance with the law.

Where a request is justified, I will take appropriate measures in relation to materials and communication channels under my control.

Where content has already been copied or shared further by third parties, it may not always be possible to remove it completely from every online service.

4.5 Social media

Once content is published on social media, personal data may be further processed by the platform operators under their own rules.

Details are available in the privacy policies of the relevant services.
